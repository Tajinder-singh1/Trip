Start with a place you love. Travel costs vary by season, departure city, hotel choice and group size—use the planner below for a rough starting estimate.

Nature · Lakes

Srinagar, Kashmir

Dal Lake, Mughal gardens and mountain views. Best known for lake experiences and scenic landscapes.

Suggested stay: 3–5 days₹₹–₹₹₹
Mountains · Adventure

Manali, Himachal Pradesh

Mountain scenery, cafés and outdoor activities. Check road and weather conditions before travelling.

Suggested stay: 3–5 days₹₹–₹₹₹
Heritage · Culture

Jaipur, Rajasthan

Explore Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort, local markets and Rajasthan’s architectural heritage.

Suggested stay: 2–3 days₹₹–₹₹₹
Spiritual · Heritage

Amritsar, Punjab

Visit Sri Harmandir Sahib (Golden Temple), experience langar and explore local Punjabi food and heritage.

Suggested stay: 1–2 days₹–₹₹
Beach · Relax

Goa

Beaches, Portuguese-influenced heritage, seafood and laid-back coastal experiences.

Suggested stay: 3–5 days₹₹–₹₹₹
Backwaters · Greenery

Alappuzha, Kerala

Known for backwaters, houseboat experiences and lush waterways. Confirm boat rates with operators.

Suggested stay: 2–4 days₹₹–₹₹₹
